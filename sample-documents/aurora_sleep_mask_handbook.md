## Battery life

Battery life is measured at the default brightness setting with audio off.

- Aurora Lite: **24 hours** of continuous use; recharges to 80% in 35 minutes.
- Aurora Pro: **48 hours** of continuous use; recharges to 80% in 45 minutes.
- Aurora Pro Max: **72 hours** of continuous use; supports wireless Qi
  charging at up to 7.5 W.

## Warranty

Helix Wellness Labs offers a **12-month limited warranty** on all Aurora
Sleep Mask models. The warranty covers manufacturing defects and battery
capacity loss greater than 30% within the warranty period. The warranty
does **not** cover water damage, accidental drops, or modifications such as
disassembly.

Aurora Pro Max units purchased through the Aurora Care program receive a
**24-month extended warranty** at no extra cost.

## Sleep tracking

Aurora Pro and Pro Max include a 6-axis IMU and an infrared eye-movement
sensor. Together they detect:

- Sleep onset latency (time-to-sleep), accurate to within 90 seconds.
- REM cycles, with up to 92% agreement against polysomnography.
- Micro-awakenings longer than 18 seconds.

Tracking data is stored locally on the mask for up to 14 days, then synced
to the Aurora app over Bluetooth. Helix Wellness Labs does **not** send
sleep data to any third-party advertising network.

## Connectivity

- **Bluetooth 5.3** with iOS 15+ and Android 11+ support.
- **Audio**: 32 kHz stereo audio over BLE; works with the included Aurora
  app and any standard A2DP source.
- **Wi-Fi**: not supported on any model. Sync is Bluetooth only.

## Cleaning and care

The outer fabric is removable and machine-washable on a cold cycle. The
internal electronics module must be removed first; never submerge it.

If the mask develops a strong odour after extended use, Helix recommends
hand-washing the fabric with mild detergent and air-drying it for 24 hours
before re-attaching the electronics module.

## Return policy

Aurora masks may be returned within **30 days** of delivery for a full
refund as long as the unit shows no visible signs of wear. Return shipping
is free in the US, EU, and India; customers in other regions pay return
postage.

To start a return, email **support@helixwellnesslabs.example** with the
order number and SKU.

## Safety notes

- Do not wear an Aurora mask while operating a vehicle, even when audio is
  off. The mask blocks light and impairs peripheral vision.
- Children under 12 should not use the Pro or Pro Max models because the
  IMU haptic motors can deliver pulses that exceed pediatric guidance.
- If you experience skin irritation, discontinue use for 48 hours and
  contact support.

## Manufacturing

All Aurora masks are assembled at Helix Wellness Labs' facility in
Coimbatore, India. Battery cells are sourced from a single audited supplier
in South Korea. Each unit is individually serial-numbered; the serial is
visible on the inside of the strap near the buckle.
