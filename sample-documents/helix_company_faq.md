# Helix Wellness Labs — Frequently Asked Questions

A short FAQ used for the customer-support knowledge base. All answers are
authoritative for the Aurora product line.

---

## About the company

**Q: Where is Helix Wellness Labs based?**
Helix Wellness Labs is headquartered in Bengaluru, India, with a
manufacturing facility in Coimbatore and an R&D office in Lisbon, Portugal.

**Q: When was the company founded?**
Helix Wellness Labs was founded in 2021 by Dr. Meera Ranganathan and
Tomás Almeida.

**Q: How many employees does Helix have?**
As of 2026, Helix has 184 full-time employees across three offices.

## Ordering and shipping

**Q: How long does shipping take?**
Standard shipping is 3-5 business days within India, 5-7 business days in
the EU and US, and 10-14 business days in other regions. Express shipping
is available for an additional $19.

**Q: Do you ship internationally?**
Yes. Helix ships to 38 countries. A full list is available on
helixwellnesslabs.example/shipping.

**Q: Is gift wrapping available?**
Yes — gift wrapping is free during the holiday season (Nov 1 to Jan 5) and
$4.99 outside that window.

## Payments and discounts

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, UPI, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal.
Helix does not currently accept cryptocurrency payments.

**Q: Are there student or healthcare discounts?**
Yes. Verified students and healthcare workers receive 15% off all Aurora
models through the SheerID portal.

**Q: How does the Aurora Care program work?**
Aurora Care is a $9/month subscription that includes a 24-month extended
warranty on Pro Max units, free annual fabric replacement, and priority
24-hour support response.

## Support hours

Customer support is available Monday through Saturday, 9 AM to 9 PM IST.
Emergency support for medical-grade Pro Max users is available 24/7 at
support-urgent@helixwellnesslabs.example.
